Her Social Network

The digital home for a women's empowerment CIC in Luton & Bedfordshire — built to inform, engage, and grow their community.

The Problem

Her Social Network CIC was running real-world programmes — workshops, mentorship, wellbeing sessions — but had no professional digital presence to match the quality of their work. Women who discovered them online had no clear way to understand what was on offer, register for events, or stay connected with the organisation.

The Build

I designed and built a full site centred on four programme pillars: Personal Growth & Confidence, Economic Independence, Community Connection, and Health & Wellbeing. The site includes an upcoming events and workshops section, a newsletter sign-up with backend integration so the team can grow their list, a community impact counter, and a contact section for direct engagement. The design system uses a triadic colour palette — Warm Rose, Soft Orchid, and Champagne Gold — to reflect the organisation's character without the aggressiveness of generic charity pinks.

The Outcome

Her Social Network now has a live, professional web presence that represents the quality and intention of their work. The site is live at hersocialnetwork.co.uk — giving the organisation a credible digital base to point to in outreach, funding applications, and community building.
