Mohair Luxury Wigs

A full e-commerce system for a luxury wig brand — product catalogue, cart, Stripe checkout, and admin order management, all built from scratch.

The Problem

Mohair is a premium wig brand that needed more than a product listing page. They needed a complete commerce infrastructure — a browsable catalogue with filters, a persistent cart, a reliable checkout with Stripe, and a backend where they could manage orders and inventory without engineering support. Off-the-shelf platforms were either too expensive, too generic, or couldn't be customised to match the brand's premium feel.

The Build

I built the entire system from the ground up using Next.js 16 and Prisma on a Supabase Postgres database. The frontend covers a filterable product gallery, individual product pages, a full cart system with persistent state, and a Stripe-powered checkout flow complete with payment intent creation and webhook handling. On the backend, an admin dashboard gives the client visibility into orders and the ability to manage product listings. Firebase handles auth; Nodemailer sends transactional order confirmation emails.

Protected admin panel for viewing and managing orders — built so the client's team can operate it without a developer in the room.

Transactional Email
