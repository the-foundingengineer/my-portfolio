Labs TAIKO Chain

toldya

Bet your friends on anything — an AI oracle settles it on-chain. No house, no AMM.

The Problem

Centralized prediction markets are heavyweight — approval processes, liquidity pools, share pricing. A casual bet between friends ("Tom can't finish a beer in 30 seconds") needs something far lighter, with no approval gate and no complex market maker.

The Build

A Foundry/Solidity escrow contract (ToldyaHub) lets anyone create a YES/NO market with a deadline and an initial stake; friends deposit TAIKO into either pool until the deadline locks. A Next.js/wagmi/viem frontend handles the UI. An AI oracle service reads the question, resolution criteria, and any submitted evidence, then posts the verdict on-chain. A flat 1% fee funds oracle gas and discourages spam markets.
