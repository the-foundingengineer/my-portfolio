Kbee Beauty Studio

A photography booking site built to turn casual browsers into paying session clients for a UK family & maternity photographer.

The Problem

Temi — the photographer behind Kbee Beauty Studio — was doing exceptional work for families, newborns, and maternity clients across the UK. But her online presence wasn't converting. Potential clients had no easy way to browse her work, understand what sessions were available, or book directly. Bookings were still coming through DMs and word-of-mouth alone.

The Build

I built a clean, premium site structured around the client journey — from discovering Temi's work in the gallery, to understanding what sessions she offers (family, maternity, newborn, make-up, wall art), to booking directly. The testimonials section surfaces genuine client reviews front and centre to build trust before a visitor even reaches the booking page. The site is fully responsive and deployed on Vercel.

What's Inside

Gallery

Curated portfolio showcasing family, kids, maternity, and make-up sessions — the primary conversion tool on the site.

Session Booking

Dedicated booking page with session types and wall art plans — clients can see exactly what they're booking before they commit.

Testimonials

Real client reviews from families and maternity clients surface the warmth and quality of Temi's sessions.

Wall Art Plans

A dedicated upsell section showcasing wall art packages — turning a one-time session into a longer-term purchase.

The Outcome

Kbee Beauty Studio is live at kbeebeautystudio.com. Temi now has a professional digital storefront that reflects the quality of her photography — giving prospective clients a clear path from discovery to booking, without the friction of back-and-forth DMs.
