Labs Concept — Not Yet Built

Paywise

Crowdsourced, real-time local commodity prices — so buyers and sellers stop guessing what's fair.

This is a concept, not a shipped product. No code has been built yet — it's included here as an honest look at an idea worth pursuing, and it's open to being built for the right partner.

The Problem

In many local markets, prices for everyday commodities — food staples, building materials, produce — are opaque and shift street by street. Buyers routinely overpay because they have no benchmark, and sellers can't prove they're pricing fairly.

The Idea

A lightweight app where everyday users submit real prices they see or pay, tagged by item and location. Aggregated and shown as a live, location-based price trend — a shared reference point instead of a guess.
