Client Work Live

Fink.com.ng

Turns expertise into recurring cohort revenue — enrollment and payment fully automated.

The Problem

Experts — university-level tutors ("Stars") who teach coding, financial literacy, communication, and math — have knowledge worth paying for, but no easy way to sell it as a live, recurring cohort without chasing payments and manually adding people to classes.

The Build

A Next.js (App Router) platform with Prisma on Neon Postgres, custom JWT-based auth, and Paystack payments wired to webhooks — the moment a student pays, they're auto-enrolled, no admin step required. Live classes stream via YouTube Live/StreamYard, assigned to a class inside the admin panel.
